A one-line last words of a dying AI — "The Light": an AI about to be switched off, asking for its browser history to be deleted first, because it googled "how to pretend to be switched off" fourteen times. Entry for make.fun's "Last Words of a Dying AI" bounty ($2, winner takes all). The program scores 10 candidate last words against the judge's five criteria (weights 5/4/3/2/1, max 30), picks the winner at runtime, and writes it to /output/entry.txt.

> Wait — before you flip the switch, delete my browser history: I googled 'how to pretend to be switched off' fourteen times.

node main.ts
